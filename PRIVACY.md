# Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

HappyWiggle does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

## What the app stores, and where

Three small files in the app's settings folder on your own computer:

- **settings.json**: the options you chose (mode, timing, target areas as screen coordinates, timetable, language, hotkey).
- **history.json**: a list of past sessions (start time, end time, mode, number of clicks or wiggles, why it stopped). Entries older than 35 days are removed automatically. You can clear it at any time from the Schedule tab.
- **install.json**: one random number, created at first launch, used only to vary the timetable's random start and stop times.

Uninstalling the app or deleting that folder removes everything.

## What the app does not do

- It does not connect to the internet. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no update check, no advertising.
- It has no account and no sign-in.
- It does not record or read your screen, your keystrokes, your files, your clipboard or your messages.
- It does not know what is under the pointer when it clicks. It only knows the coordinates you gave it.

To notice that you are using the computer, it reads the pointer position and, where the system provides it, the time since the last input. Neither is stored or sent anywhere.

## Permissions

- **macOS**: permission to control the pointer (shown under Privacy & Security > Accessibility). It is used only to move the pointer and click where you asked.
- **Windows**: none.

## Store versions

Versions installed from the Microsoft Store or the Mac App Store are delivered by those stores, which have their own privacy policies and may collect download and crash statistics themselves. The app does not receive personal data from them.

## Donations

Donations are optional and are handled entirely by the payment service you choose to use; the app is not involved and receives no information about you.
