SoftMouseWiggler

Keeps your computer awake. Then gets out of your way.

A mouse wiggler and auto clicker for Windows and Mac. Free, open source, and it never goes online.

Download for Windows Download for Mac Read the code
Windows 10 or later. macOS 11 or later, Intel and Apple Silicon.

Two ways to stay active

Wiggle only

Moves the mouse in a short, natural drift and brings it back where it was. Or pick the invisible one-pixel nudge. It never clicks.

Click areas

Choose an exact spot, or draw an area and let it click a random point inside, at random intervals. Up to five targets.

What you get

Steps aside
Touch the mouse and it waits. It carries on a few seconds after you stop.
Stops itself
After a number of clicks, after a number of minutes, or at a time of day.
Timetable
Pick days and hours once. Start, stop and lunch break drift by a few minutes each day.
One hotkey
F8 starts and stops it from anywhere. You can change the key.
Compact bar
A slim bar that stays on top and shows the time left and when it will stop.
Careful clicks
It glides to the target, clicks only if it really got there, and puts the pointer back.
Five languages
English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese, in light or dark.

Why open source matters here

An app that moves your mouse deserves scrutiny. Every line of SoftMouseWiggler is public, the installers are built by a public script from that code, and the app contains no network code at all. It cannot read your screen, your files or your messages, and it has nothing to send them with.

Use it responsibly

SoftMouseWiggler is a tool you control. Check your employer's and IT policies before using automation on a work or managed device, and do not use it to misrepresent whether you are working.
